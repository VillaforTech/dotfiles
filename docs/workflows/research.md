# Research capture workflow

Zotero owns papers, attachments, and bibliographic metadata. Better BibTeX owns
stable citekeys and a reviewed automatic export. Obsidian owns literature notes
and synthesis; it should link to citekeys instead of duplicating the source
library.

For a new paper:

1. Import and deduplicate it in Zotero.
2. Confirm the stable citekey and attachment availability.
3. Refresh the bounded BibTeX export used by the active research repository.
4. Create a literature note from the tracked Obsidian template.
5. Link claims to experiments, synthesis, and outputs rather than storing a raw
   connector or PDF dump in Git.

Validate the flow with a disposable paper before relying on it for thesis work.
Installing Zotero or downloading the Better BibTeX extension does not by itself
prove the workflow is configured.
